# Etchloom Support

Need help with Etchloom, your account, Cloud Save, or an Etchloom Space subscription?

Email us at [mood.mirror.bot@gmail.com](mailto:mood.mirror.bot@gmail.com). Please do not include mood notes, Journal pages, or other private archive content in your message.

We aim to respond within 5 business days.
